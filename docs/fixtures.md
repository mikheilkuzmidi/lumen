# Test photos

Only these photos go into `Fixtures/`:

- photos generated by the tests themselves;
- photos released under CC0 or into the public domain, each listed below with its source;
- photos taken by the project's authors that show no one who hasn't agreed to it.

No private photos, no photos of people without their consent, and no images whose license doesn't allow redistribution.

Recorded model responses in `Tests/LumenMetaTests/Recorded/` are trimmed to what the tests need. They contain no keys, no account identifiers and no private images.
